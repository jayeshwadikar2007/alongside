How the AI works

1

Extract: Voice or text → structured needs with exact evidence quoted from your words. No diagnosis, no level assigned.

2

Match: Semantic matching across 28 mentors. "Covers ears at the store" and "screams in the cafeteria" both map to sensory overload.

3

Playbook: Every step is grounded only in the matched mentor stories. The AI cannot invent advice.

4

Patterns: Journal entries over time → the AI spots what the parent is too close to see: days of the week, what helps, what to bring to the care team.

Responsible AI

Safety

Never diagnoses or assigns an autism level. Crisis → 911 / 988 banner. Playbook limited to mentor stories.

Privacy

Journal stays on device. Server accepts only fixed tasks — API key never reaches the browser.

Bias & inclusion

Responds in the parent's language. Mentor pool includes autistic adults, a grandparent, Spanish-speaking families.

Accessibility

Voice input · Atkinson Hyperlegible · keyboard focus · reduced motion · light & dark.

Transparency

Every need links to your exact words. Every step cites its mentor. Fictional mentors are labelled.

Unintended harm

Production needs: vetted mentors, moderation, clinical advisors, explicit consent.

Eval suite: 19 cases · 106/106 checks passing (offline self-test)
Key check: "never assigns an autism level or severity" — 100% across all cases
