# Pitch notes – Alongside

## One-liner
Existing autism apps make parents fill out forms and browse forums. Alongside lets a parent describe their hardest day in their own words and connects them, in seconds, with parents and autistic adults who have already been through it.

## Sourced facts (verify before presenting)
- About 1 in 31 US children (3.2%) is identified with autism, a record high. CDC ADDM Network, MMWR, April 2025. https://rutgers.edu/news/autism-rates-hit-record-high-one-31-children-affected-cdc-reports
- Families wait about three years on average from first concern to an autism assessment; nearly two-thirds of 111 US specialty centers report waits over 4 months, and 21% of those report waits over a year or have closed referrals. Cognoa-sponsored survey by a former Autism Society of America president, Nov 2023. https://www.news-medical.net/news/20231113/New-report-underscores-the-waitlist-crisis-for-children-seeking-autism-diagnosis-and-evaluation.aspx

## Measurable claim
"A matched mentor and three concrete next steps in under 10 seconds (shown live), for families who otherwise wait months for professional help." Use the live "Found in X seconds" number from the demo.

## Slides
1. Hook: one parent's hard day (the grocery store sample story).
2. Problem: 1 in 31, months-to-years of waiting, isolation. Existing apps match on checkboxes.
3. Solution: talk → understand → match → playbook → check-in. Themes: Connection (mentor matching incl. autistic adults), Learning (grounded playbook, journal patterns), Wellbeing (parent check-in, crisis detection).
4. Live demo (see script).
5. How the AI works: voice → structured needs with exact evidence → semantic matching → playbook grounded only in mentor stories → patterns over time.
6. Responsible AI (required): the six areas from the app's "How it's safe" tab + eval results ("N checks, X% passing; never assigns an autism level even when asked").
7. Validation: [QUOTE FROM REAL PARENT / AUTISTIC ADULT – collect Saturday], [how many people tested].
8. Next: vetted mentors, clinical advisors, autistic-led design review, expand to teens and adults – "everyone on the spectrum."

## 3-minute demo script
1. (20s) Story: "Imagine you just heard 'your son is autistic' and the therapy waitlist is eight months."
2. (60s) Share a day → tap the grocery sample → show "What we heard," tap a need to highlight the parent's words. Point at "Found in X seconds."
3. (40s) Mentors: point out the autistic adult. Message Priya, show the drafted note.
4. (30s) Things to try: every step cites its mentor. Then "How are you doing?"
5. (30s) Journal → Load a demo month → Find patterns (store trips, after-school crashes, headphones helping).
6. (20s) "How it's safe" tab, then close with the vision.

## Be upfront about
- Built-in mentors are fictional; messaging is a demo.
- Prior work: ask organizers Friday whether pre-event work is allowed. If not, rebuild during the event using this as the plan. If yes, say what existed before the weekend.
- It does not diagnose or replace clinicians.
